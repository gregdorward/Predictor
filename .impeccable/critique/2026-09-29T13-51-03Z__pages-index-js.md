## Overall Impression

The homepage knows what it is—a pre-match research product—but the **first screen sells marketing while hiding the terminal**. The biggest win is making today's fixtures feel **present** without another full client bundle surprise, then cutting copy weight above the skip control.

## What's Working

1. SSR `todayLinks` and real screenshot tie the page to live hub data.
2. `DeferredApp` + signed-in auto-load balances SEO, guests, and members.
3. Reduced-motion respect and descriptive screenshot alt text.
